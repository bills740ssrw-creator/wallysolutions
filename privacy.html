Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

This is a plain-language draft that describes how this website actually works today. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Legal review is pending.

What this website collects

Nothing automatically. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no advertising or tracking scripts. The fonts and images on this site are served from this domain, not from a third party.

If you email us

We receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write to us. That message is stored in our email provider and is used to reply to you and, if we work together, to run the project.

What we do with it

  • Reply to your enquiry.
  • Deliver and administer work you have asked us to do.
  • Keep records we are required to keep by law.

We do not sell your information. We do not add you to a mailing list without asking.

Who else sees it

Only the services needed to run the business, such as our email host and our accountant, or a public authority where the law requires it. We do not share your details with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept while the conversation is active and for a reasonable period after that. Records connected to paid work are kept for as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete anything that we are not legally required to keep.

Your choices

Write to hello@wallysolutions.com to ask for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted.

Links to other sites

This site links to client websites and Instagram profiles. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

Children

This site is meant for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@wallysolutions.com.
